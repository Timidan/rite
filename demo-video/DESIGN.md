# Rite Demo Video

## Style Prompt

A bright verification instrument in motion: warm paper, flat graphite structures, compact diagnostic labels, and cobalt signals traveling through authorization paths. The composition feels like a precise bench tool rather than a generic SaaS promo. Copy stays short; code, identifiers, and measured output use monospace while all product language uses a humanist sans-serif stack.

## Colors

- Warm paper: `#faf8f3`
- Instrument panel: `#fffefb`
- Graphite ink: `#1c1f26`
- Measured gray: `#4f5562`
- Calibration line: `#d8d2c4`
- Signal cobalt: `#1f3ff0`
- Cobalt tint: `#e6ebff`
- Fault coral: `#c42b12` — failure evidence only

## Typography

- Product voice: `Seravek, Avenir Next, Segoe UI, Ubuntu, Cantarell, Calibri, system-ui, sans-serif`
- Measurements: `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Cascadia Code, JetBrains Mono, Menlo, Consolas, monospace`

## Motion

Mechanical push transitions connect workflow steps. Elements enter in evidence order. Cobalt pulses move only along verified paths. Motion is confident and restrained, with no decorative bounce.

## What NOT to Do

- No dark tech canvas, neon glow, glass, gradients, or diffuse shadows.
- No coral decoration; coral is reserved for failed evidence.
- No oversized rounded cards or repeated generic card grids.
- No prose-heavy scenes or invented product metrics.
- No monospace headings or navigation labels.
